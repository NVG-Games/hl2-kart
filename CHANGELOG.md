# Changelog

All notable changes to Half-Life 2: Kart. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org):
`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` (while in 0.x, MINOR is a feature release and PATCH a fix).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The bathtub has thrusters now: two propane tanks along its sides at the back, Garry's Mod
  style, with jets that follow the throttle, go long and blue-white on a boost and roar like
  a rocket.
- The supercharged gravity gun, the Citadel's blue one, is a new item, mostly for the back of
  the race. It blasts every kart in a wide cone in front of you high into the air (they keep
  little of their speed and can come down off the road), sets off barrels and bursts
  watermelons.
- Xen comes alive. Controllers from Half-Life float over the void beside the first root bridge
  and the arch; once in a while one winds up, an orange glow growing between its hands, and
  throws a slow orange energy ball at the road where a kart will be. An orange ring marks the
  spot and your HUD shows the lock: change your line and it bursts behind you, hold it and you
  spin. Bullsquids sit at the edges of the plateau and the bay, turn to the karts coming and
  spit acid in a high arc; it leaves a glowing green puddle on the road for a few seconds, and
  driving through it spins you. All of them move with Half-Life's own animations.
- Antlions sleep under the beach sand. Keep your wheels on the boards and the road and they stay
  down; drive out onto the sand for more than a moment and the ground starts to rumble: the sand
  bulges and dust rises where they are about to come up, a ring marks where each one will land
  and your HUD shows the lock. A second later one to three antlions dig out ahead of you, run at
  where you are heading and pounce; one that reaches you spins you out. Get back onto the boards
  in time and they land behind you, stand a moment and dig back in. They never come onto the
  road, a group bites you once at most on a pass, and a Combine thumper keeps the sand around it
  quiet, thumping away.
- A dock crane stands on Highway 17's quay with a big magnet on its cable. Every few
  seconds its cab turns and swings the magnet out over the road; the magnet switches on with a
  clank and a hum, a glowing yellow ring marks the spot under it, your HUD shows the lock, and it
  comes down to just over the karts' roofs. Drive under it while it is down and it grabs your
  kart, lifts it, carries it a few metres on as the boom swings and drops it back on the road with
  a spin. Steer round the ring, or wait for it to go back up. It grabs the same kart only once
  a pass, and a gravity gun's beam can still pull a kart off it.
- Highway 17's rescue is the rebels' crane: when you fall off or get stuck, its magnet comes down
  out of the sky on its cable, clamps onto your kart and lifts it, and in a warm flash you hang
  over the road under it; it sets you down on your wheels and is wound back up.
- A new track, Nova Prospekt: the Combine prison on its sea cliff, on two storeys. From the entry
  yard through the gatehouse into a cell block's tall ground floor, the tiled canteen and up a
  long ramp onto the upper level: walkways over both yards, round the depot's roof and along the
  gallery of a ruined cell block, then down into the collapsed yard over the antlion caves, out
  along the cliff road between the outer wall and the fence, the sea far below, and back in
  through the laundry. Shortcuts: the upper tier along the cell block's wall (it drops you back
  onto the floor at its end) and a ventilation duct across the depot's roof.
- Nova Prospekt's boss, the antlion guard, and the antlion caves under the prison. The guard
  bursts up through the collapsed yard's floor and charges whoever comes down the ramp; it roars,
  the ground shakes, a ring marks where it will ram and your HUD shows the lock. If it catches you
  it shoves you across the slab into the hole where the floor gave way, and you drop into Episode
  Two's antlion caves: glowing grubs, hive nests, web sacks and stalactites, a hall of grubs and
  sleeping antlions. Nobody fishes you out: drive out yourself, up the miners' tunnel and a rock
  cutting onto the cliff road, and you come out about level with where you would have been, with
  a long boost and, holding no item, one of the strongest items over the others. Get stuck down
  there and the Combine teleport puts you back on the road. Two more holes, in the gallery's
  floor and on the yard's far side, take whoever drives too wide; hazard stripes mark each hole's
  edge and a wall stands round it. The guard comes out on every difficulty (slower and with a
  longer warning on EASY). Combine floor turrets guard the canteen, and the prison is dressed: its
  other blocks stand round the yards, cell doors, bunks, tables, dryers, signs and Breen's screens,
  light masts, ruins and antlion mounds, with prison guards watching from the yards and the
  depot's roof.
- Nova Prospekt's rescue is a Combine teleport, in the prison's cold blue-white. Two rings of
  blue light mark the ground under your kart like a teleport pad, two hoops of energy come up
  round it, leaning and turning like a gyroscope with lights racing round them, and arcs crackle
  onto the kart as the machine winds up. Then the hoops spin into a ball, a white-blue bubble
  swells inside it with the suck-in, it bursts in a blue flash and you stand on the road inside
  the bubble, which collapses onto you as the hoops are flung out and fade. R calls it there too.
- A new track, White Forest: Episode Two's forest on the way to the White Forest base, a lap of
  2.7 km on broken asphalt and logging roads through the pines. From the start beside the railway
  down into a rock cutting under the railway's girder bridge, along the gorge road onto the see-saw
  bridge over the river gorge, round the east hill and down the valley under the high bridge the
  Combine column marches over (twice, round a hairpin at the valley's end), through a cutting in
  the ridge, along the river, through the sawmill's yard and over the mill bridge, then up the
  west bank and over the railway at the level crossing. Shortcuts: the logging trail through the
  pines, with a jump over a dry gully, and straight through the sawmill's long shed.
- White Forest's trains, see-saw and Combine column. A freight train runs the main line from the
  south tunnel to the north one. At the level crossing on the west bank the lamps flash and the
  bell rings, the striped barrier arms come down well before the train and the road is shut for
  about five seconds; it never holds up the same kart two laps running, and computer drivers
  stop short of the arms too and wait until they are up again. Then the same train races you up the start straight behind its fence and
  rumbles over the girder bridge above the rock cutting. The see-saw bridge over the gorge,
  Episode Two's span on its pivot, tips toward whichever end the karts are on: you climb it as
  it rises and ride it down as it swings past level. The Combine
  column marches across the high bridge over the valley; where the lap passes under it, it halts,
  steps up to the railing, paints glowing red strips on the road where karts will be, raises its
  rifles and fires a moment later.
  Change your line and you are through. The forest is dressed: pines along the roads and the tree
  line on the hills, standing freight trains on the sidings and a derailed wagon, the cars that
  fell with the old bridge lying in the river under the see-saw, the sawmill's log stacks, rocks
  and fallen trees, the rebels' camp; rebels and vortigaunts watch, Combine soldiers guard a
  barricade under the column's bridge, and the wind blows in the pines.
- White Forest's rescue is the vortigaunts. When you fall off, sink or get stuck, three of them
  step out of green flashes beside you, rear back as their hands charge and crackle, then thrust
  them at your kart and hold it in lime-green beams, with arcs wrapping round it as it rises off
  the ground. In a green-yellow flash you are on the road among them, hanging in their beams;
  they set you down on your wheels, one of them says a word and they vanish. R calls them.

### Changed
- The houndeye packs pace up and down the verge now, and when a kart comes they run out to meet
  it, up to the road's edge but never onto it, before they charge and blast as before. Keep to
  the far side of the road when they chirp and the blast still cannot reach you.
- The gravity gun pulls now instead of punting. Its beam grabs the nearest kart ahead (up to
  60 m), lifts it, carries it back over your head and drops it just behind you, spinning:
  the two of you swap places. With nobody in reach it only clicks.
- The scanner rescue is a photo now. The scanner flies in to hang in front of your kart, its eye
  blinking as it takes aim, and photographs you: the screen goes white, and as it fades you are
  back on the road with the scanner beside you, climbing away. Sinking, falling off an edge or
  calling it with R all end that way; other players see the flash of its camera. No more drop
  from under the scanner: you are put on your wheels and drive off as the white fades, a little
  sooner than before.
- Every track has its own rescue now. City 17 (CT17) keeps the scanner's photo, and the
  coast of Highway 17 has the rebels' crane (see Added). At Black Mesa and on Surface
  Tension a Half-Life teleport builds up round you instead: a green-yellow column of light,
  sparks spiralling in, the suck-in, then a flash, and you materialise on the road in a
  second swirl. On Xen a ball of green energy forms over you, swallows you in a green flash
  and spits you out on the road. The timing is the same everywhere, the screen flashes in the
  rescuer's colour, and R calls whichever it is.
- Dog runs with Half-Life 2's own animations now: it stands with its idle pose, walks off
  with its walk and gallops on its knuckles with its run, blended by speed. At kart speeds the
  stride tops out at three a second and the feet slide, instead of the legs blurring. The
  animations are read from your Half-Life 2 when the content is built (tools/mdl_anim.py):
  content built before this keeps the old swinging legs, which no longer blur either.
- Striders hunt any kart now, not only the leader. A strider can walk its own route beside
  the road, stepping leg by leg with its feet planted on the ground, sidestepping along the
  street with its guns on you, and halts at the next spot to crouch and fire its warp cannon
  at the nearest kart it can see. A blue laser lies on that kart first, slides to where it
  will be and stays there with the ring, so you have time to swerve. As it walks it rakes the
  road with its machine gun: a short blue laser sweep and its hunting chirp, then a line of
  impacts walking down the road towards you; drive into it and you spin. It never aims at a
  kart being rescued or just put back.
- Xen has a stretch of its own instead of the cave that rolled you round (the same trick as the
  pipes elsewhere): a bridge grown of living roots, wrapped in roots and trailing them into
  the void, climbs out to three small floating islands, and between them you hop the void in
  Xen's low gravity, floating over it in long slow arcs. Green motes rise round you there,
  columns of light come up out of the void under each hop and the air hums. Even a kart slowed
  by a hit still makes it across. A second root bridge curls up to the crystal plateau. The lap
  is about 150 m longer (2.6 km).
- CT17's route goes through the ruins of City 17 now. The Combine pipe that rolled you up the
  wall is gone (the same trick as the pipes elsewhere): out of the underpass you climb onto a
  broken viaduct over the canal, its jerseys torn out in places, and one of its spans has
  collapsed, so you drive down its sagging slab and up the next. On the boulevard toward the
  Citadel the road goes in through a hole blown in a block's facade, across its ground floor
  between the pillars and out through another, then up a ramp of rubble onto the long roof of
  a collapsed building and down its fallen floor slab. The rooftops shortcut is a long way over
  the old town's roofs instead of one jump that needed a boost: a ramp spiralling up off the
  hill road, three jumps over alleys, a run along the roofs beside the street and down a fallen
  slab into it, about as quick as the hairpins below. Drop into an alley and you try that jump
  again. The lap is about 60 m longer (2.6 km).
- CT17 is the ruined City 17 of Anticitizen One. Rubble is heaped under the collapsed span and
  along the ramp up to the fallen roof, broken edges hang round the holes blown in the block
  and soot blackens its facades, craters, burnt-out cars and the burnt hull of an APC line the
  road, rubble and junk shut the side streets, most street lamps are dead, and gutted and
  fallen-in blocks and the destroyed dome stand on the skyline under the Citadel. Fires burn
  in the wrecks, the heaps and the hall of the blasted block, and columns of smoke rise from
  burning buildings all over the city. Three striders walk the route instead of the one that
  stood at the canal corner: on the inside of the viaduct's bend, between the canal jump and
  the canal corner, and beside the flyover's way down to the boulevard. Refugees and rebels
  watch the race now, fewer of them.
- Highway 17 is the coast of Half-Life 2's Highway 17 and Sandtraps now instead of City 17 and
  the canal (CT17 has the city). From the cliff-top highway you cross an inlet of the sea on the
  long bay bridge, where the gunship strafes you, and dive into the headland: a concrete portal,
  then a bore where the road rolls all the way round, over the ceiling, and out above the sea. A
  cliff road winds down to the docks and the rebels' magnet crane, a wooden bridge crosses the
  inlet's mouth onto the antlion beach, where the boardwalk is the only safe road (run off the
  planks onto the sand and you slow down and wake the antlions), and the coast road climbs the
  east cliff back to the highway. The shortcuts are the old coast bridge straight across the
  inlet, its middle span fallen into the water (jump it; short of the far side you swim), and
  the stepping stones, boards laid straight along the shore with sand between every one. The
  checkpoint's force field and the hopper mines are gone. The lap is about 500 m longer (2.6 km).
- Highway 17's coast looks lived in. Rusted wrecks line the highway, some still smoking, with
  the burnt-out APC of a Combine roadblock before the bay bridge and a rebel barricade at the
  east end; pylons stride over the hills, Highway 17's big bridge stands far up the inlet's
  valley and the old bridge's fallen span lies in the water under its gap. A trawler rides in
  the harbour by an idle dock crane, the rebels sit round a fire at their camp behind the quay
  (Gnome Chompski is with them), antlion hills rise over the dunes, driftwood and boats lie
  along the tide line, a trawler lies beached under the east cliff and a lighthouse stands on
  its point. Rebels cheer from the containers and their camp, refugees from the dunes, and
  seagulls circle all along the coast.
- Online, everyone needs this version (network protocol 4).
- NEW RACE lays the track cards out in two rows (seven tracks: four over three), a little
  shorter than before, so every track fits beside its description in any window. The arrow keys
  and a gamepad move along a row and stop at its end, down from the cards to START RACE, and up
  from START RACE to the track you picked.
- The road no longer rolls round a pipe at Black Mesa or on Surface Tension: only Highway 17's
  tunnel through the headland keeps that trick, and each of the two has a stretch of its own
  instead. At Black Mesa the tram tunnel runs into the Anomalous Materials accelerator's service
  tunnel, flat and fast: the beam line runs beside you behind a railing, its glowing beam pipe
  through blue magnets with copper coils and yellow quadrupoles, cooling lines along the other
  wall, and you pass under the ring magnets' yellow arches with their blue light strips. On
  Surface Tension the road leaves the HECU camp between two wing walls and runs down the dam's
  dry spillway, a concrete channel cut through the mountain, with marines and survivors
  looking down from its lips and the dam's water main on concrete saddles along one of them,
  crossing high over the channel halfway. Both laps keep their length.

### Removed
- The weather choice. It only ever changed Highway 17's sky; every circuit keeps its own
  sky now (Highway 17 the coast's daytime sky). The race setup, the
  server screens and the dedicated server (`mp_weather`, `--weather`) no longer have it.

### Fixed
- CT17's chain-link fences show their wire mesh instead of a blank sheet, and the shield
  panes of the Combine booths and barricades and the shells of Black Mesa's Xen crystals are
  see-through tinted glass instead of opaque white.
- The HEV overcharge knocks a kart over once per touch. A kart pressed against you was thrown
  again on every frame the two of you touched and could fly far off the road.
- On Xen a low rim of rock runs along the outside of the first floating island's bend: a kart
  spun or knocked over at the end of the first root bridge went straight on there and slid off
  the island.
- A kart pulled back over the finish line by a gravity gun just after crossing it no longer loses
  a lap: crossing the line forwards again gives the lap back.
- A kart the scanner puts back well behind where it stopped (out of a hazard's way) is no longer
  taken again a few seconds later for not getting back there in time.
- A long message across the middle of the screen ("WAITING FOR PLAYERS") is set smaller to fit
  instead of running off its edges.
- A track that your content does not have built yet can not be picked (in NEW RACE, CREATE SERVER,
  the lobby and the dedicated server's config) instead of racing Highway 17 under its name.
- The dedicated server's server.cfg: a comment after a value (server.cfg.example has one on every
  line) is no longer read as part of it. A server set up from the example as it was turned every
  player away with a wrong password, ran without bots and ignored the track it was given.
- The first-start setup finishes on a Half-Life 2 copy without Episode Two's content (an old
  install with no ep2 folder): the tracks are built without Episode Two's trains, trees and
  forest props instead of the setup stopping at CT17 and the game never starting.
- Online, the server passes on a track hazard's event from a player's game only when that player's
  kart set the hazard off (a tripmine's beam). A modified client could otherwise make every other
  player's trains, see-saw, Combine column or antlions do what it liked.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-12

Shortcuts, track hazards and spectators on five redesigned circuits. This release also
brings 0.1.1's first start that begins building by itself (0.1.1 never had a download of its
own). The download is the `.zip` only: 0.1.1's one-file `.exe` is gone again. Online,
everyone needs 0.2.0.

### Added
- Two shortcuts on every circuit, most of them ending in a jump that takes speed:
  - Highway 17: the headland trail, a dirt track over two crests, and the quarry jump straight
    across the flooded quarry.
  - CT17: the canal jump straight over the canal, and the rooftops, a ramp onto the old town's roofs
    and a jump over the street below that needs a suit battery or HEV boost.
  - Black Mesa: the crane gantry along the storage hall's wall, above the slime pit and the turrets
    (you drop off its end back onto the road), and the coolant jump over the broken catwalk.
  - Xen: the high root, a narrow root over the void with no rim at all, and the island hop across
    the bay over two floating rocks.
  - Surface Tension: the rim drop along the canyon's edge, and the washout jump over the river.
- Shortcut signs and hints: chevron boards before and at every fork, a DANGER sign before a risky
  shortcut's jump, and stripes and red lamps on the jump lips. The HUD names a shortcut the first
  time you come up to it, the minimap draws shortcuts as dashes, SHORTCUT! shows when you land one,
  and the results have a SHORTCUTS column (online the same on every screen).
- Missing a shortcut's jump: the scanner puts you back on its run-up for one more try when a
  standing start can still make the jump; otherwise, or after a second miss, you rejoin the main
  road. Fall into the flooded quarry and you are put down past the gap.
- Track hazards, each with a warning before it strikes:
  - Highway 17: a force-field gate at the Combine checkpoint, a gunship strafing the coast road and
    hopper mines on the dirt road.
  - CT17: a Combine train across the rail yard, a strider at the canal corner and floor turrets at
    the checkpoint.
  - Black Mesa: laser tripmines in the offices and labs, ceiling turrets in the storage hall and the
    Lambda core's discharges on the high walk.
  - Xen: houndeye packs, alien slaves throwing lightning and a tentacle by the long bridge.
  - Surface Tension: an Apache over the start straight, sentry guns in the HECU camp and mortars on
    the dam.
- Hazard rules: one run through a group of hazards costs at most one hit, and on EASY they warn
  longer and fire less often. A red chevron at the edge of the screen points at a turret or alien
  slave that has locked on you; the minimap marks hazards with triangles that blink red while they
  fire. TRACK HAZARDS turns them on or off in NEW RACE, CREATE SERVER and the lobby (online the
  host's choice counts for everyone), and `mp_hazards` / `--hazards` on a dedicated server.
- Spectators along every circuit: citizens, refugees, rebels, metrocops, Combine soldiers,
  scientists, security guards, HECU marines, vortigaunts and alien slaves. They cheer the start and
  the finish, react to hits, explosions, falls and headcrabs, and hop out of the way of a kart on an
  open road. Some know your racer: metrocops condemn Gordon and salute Dr. Breen, vortigaunts bow
  to a vortigaunt racer and the marines salute Shephard. Options > Video > Spectators: OFF, FEW or
  FULL.
- Easter eggs: the G-Man turns up now and then from the second lap, and there are more to find.
- Water and slime: drive in and you splash and sink, with a wash of colour over the screen, before
  the scanner hauls you out.
- WRONG WAY: a flashing red warning with a beep while you drive back along the road.
- Tubes: on CT17, Black Mesa, Xen and Surface Tension a stretch of road runs inside a pipe and rolls
  round it (up the wall, over the ceiling, or all the way round twice). Gravity pulls towards the
  pipe wall there and the camera rolls with the kart. Before a pipe ends you are brought back down
  to its bottom, so you leave it upright.
- Open edges: Xen's ledge down to the arch (only a low rim of rock) and its high root, the Surface
  Tension cliff road and rim road, and Black Mesa's coolant road round the core, where the railing
  is torn out in places. Run wide and you fall or swim; the scanner fishes you out.
- A second jump on Black Mesa (the storage hall) and Surface Tension (the HECU camp). CT17's jump on
  the lap is now in the rail yard; the canal jump became a shortcut.
- Seven new items: energy ball (bounces off walls), watermelon and 3x watermelon (spin out whoever
  hits them), hopper mine (jumps at whoever comes close), sawblade (cuts through everyone in front),
  headcrab (covers the faces of up to three racers just ahead for a couple of seconds; only one is
  in play at a time) and Xen teleport (140 m down the road).
- Clusters of explosive barrels on the road.

### Changed
- All five circuits are redesigned, 2.1 to 2.5 km a lap:
  - Highway 17: out through the Combine checkpoint, round the headland, over the inlet, out round a
    flooded quarry and back up the canal.
  - CT17: a figure-8 from the station plaza through a cutting and an underpass, the Combine pipe,
    the rail yard, the canal corner, the checkpoint and the old-town hairpins, over the flyover and
    back along the boulevard.
  - Black Mesa: across the reactor room twice, on the high walk past the core and round it at
    coolant level, with the offices, labs, accelerator, storage hall and waste processing between.
  - Xen: a figure-8 of floating islands with the cave tube, the chasm, an arch over the root bridge,
    the bay and the long bridge.
  - Surface Tension: the canyon, the gorge bridge, the HECU camp, the dam's water main, the cliff
    road and the dam's crest, then a viaduct over the start straight.
- More supply crate rows and boost pads: 8 to 12 of each a lap, and some on the shortcuts. No pad
  lies near the start grid.
- Item odds per race position: the nine original items keep their old proportions and 80 % of
  every position's odds; the seven new ones share the other 20 %, so they turn up now and then.
- Raised decks and walled roads have unseen walls above their barriers, and explosions and hazard
  hits throw a kart far less high, so it comes down on its own road.
- The scanner also comes for a kart stuck behind a wall (after 2 s) and catches a fall into the
  void sooner.
- Computer drivers stuck nose-first on a wall back off with the wheels turned and try once more
  the other way before the scanner comes. They no longer hold a long slide through S-bends, and
  they take a shortcut's jump only when they can reach the speed it needs.
- Dr. Breen taunts whoever he hits with a short line instead of a whole Breencast.
- Online: network protocol 3. Everyone needs this same version; a server of another version shows
  as "(different version)" in the server list and turns you away.
- Online: the start, the countdown and the race clock follow the server's clock on every PC. A
  player whose PC loads the race slowly is no longer dropped: the server waits up to 45 s, then
  starts, and the late player joins the race under way.
- A loading card covers the first frames of a race.
- Game content version 3: the first start after updating builds the game content again.

### Fixed
- The POSITION and LAP numbers sit right-aligned in their HUD boxes; the speed reads 0 during a
  rescue, and 3x items count down on their name.
- Karts no longer creep forward on the start grid during the countdown.
- Holes and seams in Black Mesa's walls and doorways that showed the empty world behind them.
- Short stalls the first time an effect or an item model showed up in a race.
- Karts thrown 10-20 m into the air by a pile-up, a wall scrape, a steep bank or several explosions
  going off at once: a hit now gives a hop of about 1.5 m at most.
- The camera keeps a kart in view while it sinks short of a jump, instead of showing the road above.
- Spectators' easter-egg lines went silent while your racer was talking; they now speak under him,
  and a line for a crowd you have already passed is dropped instead of said late.
- Online: the results order and the finish times match on every screen, and other players' karts
  no longer dip into the road when they land.
- Black Mesa: at the end of the storage hall, railings run diagonally across both side floors and
  lead you back onto the road into waste processing. You no longer get stuck in the corner by the
  toxic channels or climb the low fence there into the slime.
- Surface Tension: the HECU camp's supply crates sit just past the camp jump's landing, so nobody
  fires an item at you on the kicker, and the explosive barrels stand at the camp's entrance, before
  the sentry guns, so a kart spun there has room to get back up to speed. The guard rail on the
  cliff road meets the road's edge: a kart knocked off drops to the beach and the scanner picks it
  up at once instead of leaving it stuck beside the rail.
- Xen: the island hop's kickers are longer and smoother, so heavy rides such as the Dog no longer
  slam into the ramp and flip back before the lip; the jump needs the same speed as before. The
  landing past the chasm is much wider on the outside of the bend, so a kart hit just before the
  jump usually lands on ground.
- Xen: the G-Man now watches from a spot every racer passes, including those who take the island
  hop.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-25

  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\HL2Kart` and every run starts the game from there.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-24

The first public build.

### Added
- Five circuits of about 2 km each: **Highway 17**, **CT17** (Combine-held City 17),
  **Black Mesa** (indoors, around the Lambda reactor), **Xen** (floating islands) and
  **Surface Tension** (the desert, the gorge bridge and the dam).
- 12 racers from Half-Life 2 and Half-Life, 6 vehicles, HL2 items, drifting and turbo boosts.
- Multiplayer over LAN and the internet: server browser, lobby with chat, bots, dedicated
  server and master server.
- First-start setup inside the game: it finds Half-Life 2 and Half-Life through Steam and
  builds all the game content from them.
- Update check: the main menu shows a download button when a newer release exists
  (can be turned off in Options > Game).
- Windows download with Godot and Python included, so nothing else needs installing.
